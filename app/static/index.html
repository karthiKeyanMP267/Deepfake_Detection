3V3 Multimodal Deepfake Architecture

Agent-Assisted Cross-Modal Consistency Learning & Adaptive Fusion

Vision Feature Stream (3 Encoders)

  • EfficientNet-B0 (1280-D): Frame-level spatial texture & high-frequency synthesis artifact detection.
  • Video Swin-Tiny (768-D): 3D spatio-temporal video transformer capturing inter-frame continuity anomalies.
  • Face/Mouth ResNet-18 (512-D): 2D facial landmark & perioral articulation dynamics.

Audio Feature Stream (3 Encoders)

  • Whisper-Base (512-D): Phonetic & semantic speech representation encoding.
  • Wav2Vec2-Base (768-D): Self-supervised acoustic latent features for vocoder artifact detection.
  • Spectrogram-CNN (256-D): Mel-spectrogram time-frequency acoustic distribution analysis.

Fusion & Forensic Intelligence

  • Cross-Modal Consistency (SAV): Evaluates cosine similarity between normalized 256-D vision and audio projection heads against threshold τ = 0.1177.
  • Adaptive Fusion: Softmax attention network dynamically allocating modality weights αv and αa with trained video-only fallback.
  • Forensic Reasoning Agent: Synthesizes grounded explanations directly from detector evidence with strict anti-hallucination guardrails.
